Source & licence

The original map is 付図第五「北支那方面軍占拠地域内治安概況（昭和十七年九月中）」, an appendix to 防衛庁防衛研修所戦史室 編『北支の治安戦＜2＞』 (戦史叢書 第50巻, 東京: 朝雲新聞社, 1971).

View the scanned original sheet (JPEG, 3768×3066) — the layers on this page are traced from it.

The volume has also been scanned and can be read online at the National Institute for Defense Studies (防衛省防衛研究所): 戦史叢書第050巻 北支の治安戦＜2＞ — the map itself is 付図第五.
